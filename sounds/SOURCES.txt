KrishiNova sound module audio sources

Installed clips are limited to 5 seconds, mono, 22050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Alarm/distress recordings are prototype deterrent attempts only; they are not guaranteed to make an animal leave.

Bird
File: bird_alert.wav
Recording: "scare birds.wav"; uploader describes it as fan noise to scare birds; tagged birds/field/scare. This is practical bird-scaring audio, not a distress-call recording or a field-trial-proven dispersal system.
Creator: rodrigovaz
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0); credit the creator and link the license.
Source page: https://freesound.org/people/rodrigovaz/sounds/477542/
Installed from Freesound high-quality MP3 preview and converted to 5-second mono, 22050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Freesound's full WAV download link requires login; the preview was used for this demonstration.
Commercial documented alternative: Scarecrow B.I.R.D. system/library uses species-specific distress recordings for bird dispersal, but its call library is commercial/proprietary; no downloadable open-license sample was found. Details: https://www.scarecrow.eu/scarecrow-b-i-r-d-system/
